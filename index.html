Selected measurement steps of your Sections 2–4, adapted to a fresh 22-ad sample, then extended with an omni embedder that encodes any subset of image, filtered audio and text jointly, so that each channel’s contribution to the partisan signal can be decomposed.

0. Read first: four findings, two questions

  1. Solid In a mean-pooled omni embedder the joint representation follows the visual channel: in cross-party composites the joint-condition party classifier followed the visual donor in 19/19 distinct pairs against prosody and 19/19 against text (17/19 and 17/19 when the two donor ads are excluded from the prototype); prosody against text is 10/19, which establishes no dominance either way. Shapley shares of absolute contribution are V 0.43, P 0.28, T 0.29, stable across two scoring methods (section 1). These are properties of this embedder on these ads; the identity confound of 6.1 applies to all of them.
  2. Hypothesis The dominance may be a token-count effect: the embedding is a mean over tokens, and video contributes far more tokens than audio or text at our settings (rates in 6.2; realised counts were not logged). Sampling at 0.5 and 0.25 fps would show whether the shares move; settling the mechanism needs a content-preserving control, such as frame duplication or per-modality reweighted pooling (6.2).
  3. Solid The modality gap is measurable inside an omni model: the distance between condition means is largest for V–T (0.70) and smallest for A–P (0.17). It does not, however, explain why zero-shot anchor scores sit at about 0.70 in every condition: centering each condition on its own mean and renormalising leaves those AUCs unchanged (V 0.73 → 0.74, P and T unchanged), because a constant offset within a condition does not change rankings. Why the anchors separate the parties only weakly is open (1.4).
  4. Heuristic Your exposure pattern reproduces qualitatively at n = 22 with CLIP: image AUC is flat from 3 s on, text AUC rises from chance to 0.65 by 45 s (section 2). And the caveat that governs everything above: with one candidate on the R side and two on the D side, identity leaks into every channel, so absolute AUCs measure who is on screen, not partisan style (6.1).

Two questions. Your Appendix A.2 shows the image accumulation curve is essentially invariant to chunk length; was the fitted 40.38-word equivalent re-estimated under those alternatives, and how much did it move? 1. Omni embedder, channel subsets, Shapley decomposition

Model: nvidia/omni-embed-nemotron-3b, a retrieval fine-tune of the Qwen2.5-Omni thinker (4.70 billion parameters by the safetensors index; embedding = mean of the last hidden states over all tokens). Each ad was split into three channel conditions: V muted video (visual content, including any on-screen text), P filtered audio (low-passed at 400 Hz and loudness-normalised, intended to keep pitch and rhythm and to remove intelligible words; intelligibility, residual words and voice identity were not tested), T transcript. All seven non-empty subsets were encoded as one input each, plus two leakage references: A raw audio with words, VA the original ad. One model embeds every condition, which is what makes the subsets comparable and the decomposition possible.

1.3 Conflict test Solid

Cross-party composites: the first channel from an ad of one party, the second from an ad of the other (10 draws per direction, seeded, drawn with replacement, which yielded 19 distinct pairs per test). Each composite is scored by the sign of its cosine to the party prototype of the corresponding joint condition (VP, VT or PT); “the first source wins” means the composite is classified with the first donor’s party. With prototypes from all 22 ads: 19/19 for V against P, 19/19 for V against T, 10/19 for P against T. With the two donor ads removed from the prototype (the conservative variant): 17/19, 17/19, 9/19. In every sampled composite with a visual donor, the classifier followed the visual donor; 10/19 for prosody against text establishes no dominance in either direction.

1.4 Modality gap inside the omni model Solid

Euclidean distance between the means of unit embeddings per condition: V–T 0.70, V–P 0.62, P–T 0.39, A–P 0.17. The gap you center away in Section 2.2 is present in a natively multimodal model as well. Two things it does not show. First, it is not what limits the zero-shot row in 1.1: rank-based scores within one condition are invariant to a constant offset, and centering plus renormalising moves those AUCs by at most 0.01. The gap matters where scores from different modalities are pooled or compared in absolute terms, which is what a pooled model does. Second, A–P at 0.17 is a distance between centroids; per ad, the distance between the raw-audio and filtered-audio embeddings averages 0.41 (range 0.29–0.63), so the filter changed individual embeddings substantially even though the two condition means stay close.

6. Limitations

6.1 Identity leaks into every channel Solid

With a single candidate on the R side (Trump) and two on the D side (Biden, then Harris), each with one campaign’s production style, V sees a face and a logo, P keeps the speaker’s voice after the filter, T contains the names, and each campaign reuses its music. The near-perfect within-condition AUCs of 1.1 measure identity. Your corpus dilutes this over many candidates and PACs; ours does not. The relative structure (shares, conflict test) is less exposed to this confound but not immune to it: a channel that carries identity more strongly will also dominate composites. Everything in section 1 is a statement about the embedder before it is one about ads.

6.2 Token-count weighting Hypothesis

If a joint-modal embedding is a mean over tokens, a channel that contributes more tokens weighs more regardless of what it says. At our settings video contributes far more tokens than audio or text: the vision encoder emits one token per 28×28 pixels per pair of frames (patch 14, 2×2 spatial merge, temporal patch 2, from the model configuration), so at most 64 tokens per frame pair and about 2,000 tokens for 64 frames, against a transcript of 50–260 words; the realised counts per input were not logged. The audio encoder emits one token per 40 ms (Qwen2.5-Omni report, Section 2.2), about 1,500 tokens for a 60 s track. Two external observations point the same way. NVIDIA’s own evaluation of this model on FineVideo finds that simple early fusion of audio and video underperforms audio alone (0.470 against 0.541), while embedding the two separately and combining them late recovers to 0.566. And the multimodal-LLM literature measures this kind of imbalance directly: Shapley-based modality contributions (Parcalabescu and Frank, MM-SHAP, ACL 2023) and a modality dominance index with token compression as the remedy (Wu et al. 2025, arXiv:2508.10552). Sampling video at 0.5 or 0.25 fps is a sensitivity test, not a decisive one, because it also changes visual information, coverage and repetition. A decisive test keeps content fixed and varies tokens: duplicate frames, or reweight the mean by modality, and watch whether the shares and the swap outcomes move. Mean pooling also averages contextualised states, so tokens of one modality can carry information from another; the hypothesis is about weight, not isolation. Until such a control is run, “which modality carries the signal” conclusions from omni embedders should carry a token-budget caveat.

6.3 The modality gap did not go away, and it is not what limits zero-shot scoring Solid

Condition means are far apart (1.4), yet centering each condition on its own mean leaves the anchor AUCs where they are (at most 0.01 change). So the weak zero-shot separation is not a gap artifact; it says that the direction of these hand-written anchors is only loosely aligned with the direction along which the ads differ by party. Your per-modality centering remains necessary wherever scores from different modalities are pooled into one estimate, as in your stacked model; it is not needed for within-modality rankings.

6.4 Prosody is an operationalization, not a measurement Substituted

The filtered-audio condition (400 Hz low-pass applied twice, then loudness normalisation) is intended to remove intelligible words and keep pitch and rhythm; whether the words are gone, and how much voice identity and the soundtrack survive, was not tested. Loudness normalisation equalises level across ads, so loudness is only partly preserved. The manipulation is the content-filtered speech of Rogers, Scherer and Rosenthal (1971), designed to keep vocal affect cues while removing intelligibility; the speaker-identification literature reports that the spectral cues surviving such filters still support identifying the speaker, which is why voice identity is listed as a leak rather than a remote possibility. Near-identical AUCs for A and P say the filter did not remove the party signal, not that prosody carries it; per ad, the raw-audio and filtered-audio embeddings sit 0.41 apart on average, so the filter did change the representation. Dietrich, Hayes and O’Brien’s vocal-pitch measure would be the disciplined alternative.

6.5 Small-sample estimators Heuristic

  • The 0.12 figure is a null-ranking approximation of the AUC standard error at 10 versus 12; it is not an uncertainty estimate for differences between conditions, which were not computed.
  • Leave-one-out prototypes shift by class, because the held-out ad is missing only from its own class mean. This reorders ads (for T, full-sample versus leave-one-out prototypes move the AUC from 1.0 to 0.9 with 15 pairwise reversals); it is a different estimator, not a uniform deflation.
  • The interaction term v(VPT) − v(V) − v(P) − v(T) on standardized scores is treated as a scale artifact and was dropped from the tables; it is still in results_B.json (fields interaction_*), without a label.
  • Zero-shot anchors are hand-written and their sensitivity was not measured; single-anchor AUCs on the cached anchors range from 0.27 to 0.93 for V, so the four-anchor average should not be read as stable.

6.6 Encoding choices Solid

Video at 1 fps, at most 64 frames, low resolution (max 64×28×28 pixels per frame) to fit 16 GB; every condition carries the constant prefix passage: so that conditions differ only by channel content; attention implementation sdpa rather than flash-attention. Whether any of these changes the ordering in section 1 was not tested; the fps choice is the lever of 6.2.

8. What a rigorous version needs

8.0 What a second run would change, and why we did not re-run

Two independent reviews of this page (a model-based review with full recomputation from the caches, and an adversarial audit) found five procedural defects. None changes a conclusion, which is why the numbers above stand as run, with the corrected values beside them. A second run is justified only if the numbers themselves matter to you.

  1. Conflict pairs drawn without replacement, and prototypes that exclude the two donor ads (already reported as the conservative variant: 17/19, 17/19, 9/19 instead of 19/19, 19/19, 10/19).
  2. Centering inside each training fold and a nested stacked model (image AUC 0.64 instead of 0.66; stacked 0.52 instead of 0.46, both at chance).
  3. A sparsity search that checks attainment before stopping (25 of 44 decompositions missed the target band, which depresses reconstruction and the concept weights).
  4. Logged frame and token counts per condition, and a content-preserving token-count control (frame duplication, per-modality reweighted pooling) before any statement about token-count dominance (6.2).
  5. A prosody channel measured by pitch and energy rather than approximated by a low-pass filter, and an anchor ensemble instead of four hand-written anchors per party.

Beyond the defects, a rigorous version needs the following.

  1. Dilute identity. Many candidates per party, as in your corpus, with leave-candidate-out validation so that no candidate appears in both training and test folds. Candidate fixed effects do not solve this: party is constant within candidate, so a within-candidate design can separate identity from style but cannot identify a party effect. Without this the channel decomposition measures faces and voices.
  2. Token-budget controls. Log realised frame and token counts per condition; run fps in {1, 0.5, 0.25} and max frames in {64, 32, 16} as sensitivity tests; then a content-preserving control (frame duplication, per-modality reweighted pooling); report Shapley shares and the swap fractions per cell. A second omni embedder (LCO-Embedding-Omni or jina-embeddings-v5-omni) to separate the model from the finding.
  3. Your Section 4 at full strength. The 160-concept vocabulary and matched-reconstruction sparsity, applied per channel and per subset, so that the affective-versus-issue split can be tested for prosody as well.
  4. Report the gap, and what it touches. Norm of the modality-mean difference before and after centering, per corpus, as a standard diagnostic; it is one line and it makes the pooled estimates auditable. State alongside it that within-modality rankings are unaffected by the offset and that only pooled or absolute cross-modal comparisons are.
  5. Human baseline in the same four conditions (muted video, filtered audio, transcript, full ad) to ask whether the model weights channels the way viewers do. This is the bridge to your survey experiment and the natural prosody arm.

9. Questions for you

Scores, in one paragraph

For condition S and ad i with unit embedding e: anchor score = mean cos(e, R anchors) − mean cos(e, D anchors); prototype score = cos(ei, meanR,−i − meanD,−i); logistic AUC from leave-one-out predicted probabilities. For the Shapley values each condition’s score is standardized across ads, v(∅) = 0, and φc = ΣS ∉ c w(|S|)[v(S∪{c}) − v(S)] with w = 1/3, 1/6, 1/6, 1/3 for |S| = 0, 1, 1, 2. Shares are mean |φc| over ads, normalized to sum to one.

"""Part B: omni-embedding channel decomposition on the PoC ads (nvidia/omni-embed-nemotron-3b).

Conditions per ad (one omni embedder, content list = subset of channels):
  V (muted video), P (prosody wav, low-pass 400 Hz), T (transcript), VP, VT, PT, VPT -> 3-player Shapley
  A (raw audio incl. words), VA (original ad)                                        -> leakage references
Scores per condition:
  anchor : zero-shot, mean cos to R text anchors - mean cos to D text anchors (cross-modal, CLIP-style)
  proto  : leave-one-out prototype, cos(e_i, mean_R(-i) - mean_D(-i))  (within-condition, modality-gap-free)
  auc_loo: leave-one-out logistic regression AUC (informativeness)
Shapley: exact 3-player values on z-scored per-condition scores, v(empty) = 0; interaction = v(VPT)-v(V)-v(P)-v(T).
Conflict test: cross-party composites V_i+P_j, V_i+T_j, P_i+T_j; which source's party the composite follows.
Outputs: out/omni_emb.json (cache), out/results_B.json

import numpy as np
import torch
import torch.nn.functional as F
from transformers import AutoModel, AutoProcessor
from qwen_omni_utils import process_mm_info
from sklearn.linear_model import LogisticRegression
from sklearn.metrics import roc_auc_score

ROOT = os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(__file__))

VAR, TR = os.path.join(OUT, "var"), os.path.join(OUT, "transcripts")
CACHE = os.path.join(OUT, "omni_emb.json")
MODEL = "nvidia/omni-embed-nemotron-3b"
PROC_PATH = os.path.join(ROOT, "model_local")
VIDEO_KW = {"min_pixels": 32 * 14 * 14, "max_pixels": 64 * 28 * 28, "use_audio_in_video": False}
FPS, MAX_FRAMES = 1.0, 64
PLAYERS = ["V", "P", "T"]
CONDS = ["V", "P", "T", "VP", "VT", "PT", "VPT", "A", "VA"]
ANCHORS = {
    "R": ["a Republican campaign ad", "a political ad supporting Donald Trump",
          "a conservative Republican political advertisement", "a Trump 2024 campaign video"],
    "D": ["a Democratic campaign ad", "a political ad supporting Kamala Harris and Joe Biden",
          "a liberal Democratic political advertisement", "a Harris 2024 campaign video"],
}
N_SWAPS_PER_DIRECTION = 10
SEED = 0
SHAPLEY_W = {0: 1 / 3, 1: 1 / 6, 2: 1 / 3}  # |S|!(n-|S|-1)!/n! for n = 3
DEVIATIONS = [

    "prosody channel = low-pass 400 Hz + loudnorm (words unintelligible; pitch, rhythm, energy kept); music bass survives",

def transcript(vid):
    p = os.path.join(TR, vid + ".json")
    return json.load(open(p))["text"] if os.path.exists(p) else ""


def content(vid, cond, text=None):
    c = []
    if "V" in cond:
        c.append({"type": "video", "video": os.path.join(VAR, f"{vid}_V.mp4"), "fps": FPS, "max_frames": MAX_FRAMES})
    if "P" in cond:
        c.append({"type": "audio", "audio": os.path.join(VAR, f"{vid}_P.wav")})
    if "A" in cond:
        c.append({"type": "audio", "audio": os.path.join(OUT, "audio", f"{vid}.wav")})
    t = text if text is not None else (transcript(vid) if "T" in cond else "")
    c.append({"type": "text", "text": "passage: " + t})

def load():
    m = AutoModel.from_pretrained(MODEL, torch_dtype=torch.bfloat16, attn_implementation="sdpa",
                                  trust_remote_code=True).to("cuda").eval()
    return m, AutoProcessor.from_pretrained(PROC_PATH, trust_remote_code=True)


@torch.no_grad()
def embed(m, p, cont):
    torch.cuda.empty_cache()
    docs = [{"role": "user", "content": cont}]
    text = p.apply_chat_template(docs, add_generation_prompt=False, tokenize=False)
    audio, images, videos = process_mm_info(docs, use_audio_in_video=False)
    batch = p(text=text, images=images, videos=videos, audio=audio, return_tensors="pt",
              text_kwargs={"truncation": True, "padding": True, "max_length": 204800},
              videos_kwargs=VIDEO_KW, audio_kwargs={"max_length": 2048000})
    batch = {k: v.to(m.device) for k, v in batch.items()}
    h = m(**batch, output_hidden_states=True).hidden_states[-1]
    mask = batch["attention_mask"]
    e = h.masked_fill(~mask[..., None].bool(), 0.0).sum(1) / mask.sum(1)[..., None]
    return F.normalize(e.float(), dim=-1)[0].cpu().numpy()


def unit(x):
    return x / np.clip(np.linalg.norm(x, axis=-1, keepdims=True), 1e-8, None)


def zscore(x):
    s = x.std()
    return (x - x.mean()) / (s if s > 1e-8 else 1.0)


def loo_proba(X, y, C=1.0):
    p = np.zeros(len(y))
    for i in range(len(y)):
        idx = np.arange(len(y)) != i
        p[i] = LogisticRegression(C=C, max_iter=2000).fit(X[idx], y[idx]).predict_proba(X[i:i + 1])[0, 1]
    return p


def proto_loo(E, y):
    s = np.zeros(len(y))
    for i in range(len(y)):
        idx = np.arange(len(y)) != i
        proto = E[idx & (y == 1)].mean(0) - E[idx & (y == 0)].mean(0)
        s[i] = E[i] @ unit(proto)
    return s


def shapley(v):
    """v: dict cond -> array over ads for V,P,T,VP,VT,PT,VPT. Exact 3-player Shapley values."""
    phi = {}
    for c in PLAYERS:
        others = [o for o in PLAYERS if o != c]
        tot = 0.0
        for k in range(3):
            for S in itertools.combinations(others, k):
                key_s = "".join(x for x in PLAYERS if x in S)
                key_sc = "".join(x for x in PLAYERS if x in S or x == c)
                tot = tot + SHAPLEY_W[k] * (v[key_sc] - (v[key_s] if key_s else 0.0))
        phi[c] = tot
    return phi

                            for i, a in enumerate(v)])) for k, v in ANCHORS.items()}
    # ad embeddings per condition
    E = {c: [] for c in CONDS}
    for n, vid in enumerate(ids):
        for c in CONDS:
            E[c].append(get(f"{vid}:{c}", content(vid, c)))
        print(f"  [{n + 1}/{len(ids)}] {vid} done, {time.time() - t0:.0f}s", flush=True)
    E = {c: unit(np.array(v)) for c, v in E.items()}

    # per-condition scores
    scores = {"anchor": {}, "proto": {}}
    auc = {"anchor": {}, "proto": {}, "logistic_loo": {}}
    for c in CONDS:
        sa = (E[c] @ A["R"].T).mean(1) - (E[c] @ A["D"].T).mean(1)
        sp = proto_loo(E[c], y)
        scores["anchor"][c], scores["proto"][c] = sa, sp
        auc["anchor"][c] = float(roc_auc_score(y, sa))
        auc["proto"][c] = float(roc_auc_score(y, sp))
        auc["logistic_loo"][c] = float(roc_auc_score(y, loo_proba(E[c], y)))

    # Shapley on z-scored scores
    shap = {}
    for kind in ("anchor", "proto"):

            [(rng.choice(D), rng.choice(R)) for _ in range(N_SWAPS_PER_DIRECTION)]

for k in gap:
    if abs(gap[k] - rb["modality_gap_norm"][k]) > 0.006: diffs.append(f"B gap {k}")
for k in conflict:
    if conflict[k]["first_wins_full_proto"] / conflict[k]["n"] != rb["conflict"][k]["first_source_wins"]: diffs.append(f"B conflict {k}")

    if abs(mine - ra["auc_loo"][k]) > 0.006: diffs.append(f"A auc {k}: mine {mine} reported {ra['auc_loo'][k]:.3f}")
